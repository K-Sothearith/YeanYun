# YeanYun (យានយន្ត)

**Keep every vehicle's maintenance organized, even without internet.**

YeanYun is an offline vehicle-maintenance companion that helps owners keep track of the care their vehicles need. Whether you ride a motorcycle, drive a conventional car or an electric vehicle, or manage a truck, YeanYun brings maintenance schedules, service records, mileage readings, and expenses together in one place.

## Why YeanYun?

Maintenance is easy to overlook when service dates, odometer readings, and receipts are scattered across notebooks or forgotten altogether. Missed maintenance can lead to inconvenient breakdowns, unexpected costs, and uncertainty about what work was done last.

YeanYun makes it easier to **know what needs attention, when it is due, and what it has cost**—without relying on an internet connection or online account.

## What you can do

- **Manage multiple vehicles.** Keep separate profiles for motorcycles, conventional cars, electric vehicles (including battery-electric, extended-range, and plug-in hybrid vehicles), and trucks.
- **Plan maintenance.** Create tasks such as oil changes, brake inspections, tire checks, or vehicle-specific servicing. Set a distance interval, a time interval, or both when appropriate.
- **Get local reminders.** See upcoming and overdue maintenance and receive device notifications, subject to notification permissions and device settings.
- **Record completed work.** Save service dates, odometer readings, notes, and costs to build a useful maintenance history.
- **Track mileage and spending.** Review odometer updates and maintenance expenses for each vehicle.
- **Start from suggested tasks.** Use maintenance templates as a starting point, then confirm which tasks apply and adjust intervals using your vehicle's official guidance.
- **Keep control of your data.** Store records on your device and export/import a local backup when needed.

## Designed to work offline

YeanYun is designed for everyday use **without a network connection**. Vehicle information, maintenance records, schedules, and expense history are stored locally. You do not need to create an account to manage your vehicles.

## Who is it for?

YeanYun is intended for individual vehicle owners, people who use several kinds of vehicles, and anyone who wants a straightforward record of routine vehicle care.

## Important notes

- Maintenance templates are **suggestions, not manufacturer-approved schedules**. Always consult your owner's manual or qualified service provider.
- Distance is recorded in **kilometres**. The initial release uses **one user-selected reporting currency** for recorded expenses; it does not convert between currencies.
- YeanYun is a maintenance organizer, not a diagnostic tool. It does not connect to vehicle electronics or automatically read the odometer.
- The project does not include photo uploads, fuel-consumption tracking, or spare-parts inventory.

## Project status

**In development.** This README describes the intended application; not every feature is implemented yet.

## Technology

YeanYun is being built as a Flutter mobile application with on-device storage, local notifications, and automated tests. The application is designed to operate entirely offline.
